iPhone

Feels like the big streaming apps. Plays your files.

A native SwiftUI app with a home screen, search, a library and a player that takes its colors from the cover. Lossless stays lossless, and the whole library works offline if you want it to.

Listening

  • Endless radio from a song, artist, genre or your favorites. It plays more of what you finish or like and less of what you skip.
  • Autoplay keeps going with similar songs when an album or playlist ends.
  • Crossfade of up to 12 seconds, a sleep timer that fades out, and volume leveling per song or album.
  • A queue you reorder by dragging, swipe gestures in the player, synced lyrics.
  • Artist pages with popular songs, the discography, “Fans also like” and a biography.

Offline

  • Downloads of songs, albums and playlists, five at a time in the background, optionally on Wi-Fi only.
  • Library sync puts every album, artist and playlist on the phone, so it opens instantly, even in a tunnel.
  • Loading ahead: the next one to five songs are fetched early, so a dead spot doesn't stop the music.
  • Lyrics come along, and scrobbles made offline are sent later.

Sound quality

  • Set separately for Wi-Fi, cellular and downloads.
  • Original files: FLAC stays FLAC. Or let your server convert to AAC, Opus or MP3.
  • Each level shows how much data an hour of music takes.
  • Formats the iPhone can't play are converted automatically.

Your server, your way

  • Home address: on your Wi-Fi the app talks to the server directly and switches back outside, mid-song and without a gap.
  • Custom HTTP headers per account, for Cloudflare Access, Authelia and similar. Secrets stay in the Keychain.
  • Several accounts, with a “Who's listening?” picker on start.
  • A heart for the lock screen and Control Center, and a Siri shortcut to like the current song.

Make it yours

  • Eight accent colors or your own, and nine app icons.
  • Choose the start screen and which rows the home screen shows.
  • Backgrounds tinted with the cover, or plain black.
  • Pin playlists to the top of your library with a long press.

Small things

  • The quality badge in the player shows what is playing, like FLAC or AAC 256.
  • Swipe the mini player to skip, swipe up to open the player.
  • Smart playlists from your server are marked and kept read-only.
  • Logging out removes that account's downloads and cache from the phone.

Radio

Endless radio, and your stations live.

Pick a song and Tonalo keeps playing music like it, straight from your library, for as long as you listen. It gets better the more you skip and like.

The internet radio stations set up in Navidrome play live on both apps. On the iPhone you see the song on air, and if it's in your library, one tap plays it from there.

Between devices

Start on the train, finish on the couch.

Your queue and position are kept on your Navidrome server. Pause on the iPhone, open Tonalo on the TV, and it offers to carry on with the same song at the same second. It works the other way round too.

No extra service and no pairing. It uses Navidrome's own play queue, so there is nothing to set up.

Apple Watch & CarPlay

On your wrist and in the car.

CarPlay

  • Home, library and downloads
  • Shuffle, repeat and a heart in the player
  • No search, because Apple doesn't allow a keyboard in CarPlay music apps

Privacy

Your music stays between you and your server.

Tonalo has no backend of its own. There is no account to create and nothing to opt out of.

No password stored

Only the salted token the Subsonic API uses is kept, never the password itself.

Keychain on the iPhone

The token and any custom headers live in the iOS Keychain, on that one device.

No analytics

No tracking, no ad SDKs, no third-party code at all in the iPhone app.

Headers stay put

Custom headers never follow a redirect to another host.

https by default

Plain http is only used for addresses in your home network.

Open code

Every line is on GitHub, so you can check what the apps do.

The only other connection: a radio station's own server while you listen to it. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Good to know

Which servers does it work with?

Tonalo is built and tested with Navidrome. Other servers with the Subsonic API, like Gonic or Airsonic-Advanced, should work but haven't been tried yet. Reports are very welcome.

Does it cost anything?

No. Both apps are free and MIT licensed, without ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

When will it be in the App Store and the Homebrew Channel?

The iPhone app is in public beta on TestFlight right now; the App Store comes next. The TV app is planned for the Homebrew Channel. Until then, the .ipk from the GitHub releases works on any rooted TV or one in Developer Mode.

Why are artist pages empty on my server?

Popular songs, similar artists and biographies come from Navidrome, which gets them from Last.fm. Adding a free Last.fm API key to Navidrome takes two minutes; the server tips show how. Without it, the iPhone app shows your own most played songs instead.

Is there an Android or Samsung TV version?

Not at the moment. Tonalo is made for LG webOS and the iPhone.

Was AI involved?

A good part of the code was written together with an AI coding assistant. Everything is tested on a real LG OLED and iPhone, and the code is public, so you can see exactly what it does.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Open an issue on GitHub; there are forms for both. On the iPhone you can also send a screenshot as TestFlight feedback. Security problems please report privately.
