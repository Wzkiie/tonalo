Windows 10 and 11

The same app on your PC.

Tonalo for Windows is the TV app with everything it can do, made for mouse and keyboard. Same screens, same profiles, same endless radio, and your iPhone controls it just like the TV.

Mouse and keyboard

Click anywhere in the progress bar, right-click for options, Space to play and pause, Ctrl + F to search. Ctrl + Plus and Minus make everything bigger or smaller.

Volume and media keys

A volume slider in the player and in full screen, also for radio stations. Media keys and the Windows media overlay show what's playing.

Updates inside the app

Tonalo checks GitHub for a new release, asks, downloads it and restarts. No admin rights needed, it installs just for you.

iPhone

Feels like the big streaming apps. Plays your files.

A native SwiftUI app with a home screen, search, a library and a player that takes its colors from the cover. Lossless stays lossless, and the whole library works offline if you want it to.

Listening

  • Endless radio from a song, artist, genre or your favorites. It plays more of what you finish or like and less of what you skip.
  • Autoplay keeps going with similar songs when an album or playlist ends.
  • Crossfade of up to 12 seconds, a sleep timer that fades out, and volume leveling per song or album.
  • A queue you reorder by dragging, swipe gestures in the player, synced lyrics.
  • Artist pages with popular songs, the discography, “Fans also like” and a biography.

Offline

  • Downloads of songs, albums and playlists, five at a time in the background, optionally on Wi-Fi only.
  • Library sync puts every album, artist and playlist on the phone, so it opens instantly, even in a tunnel.
  • Loading ahead: the next one to five songs are fetched early, so a dead spot doesn't stop the music.
  • Lyrics come along, and scrobbles made offline are sent later.

Audiobooks and podcasts

  • Audiobookshelf in the same app: connect your server under Settings, your progress stays in sync.
  • Chapters, playback speed, skip buttons and a sleep timer.
  • Downloads for listening offline; your position is sent later.
  • Pages for authors and series, and hearts that fill your library.

Sound quality

  • Set separately for Wi-Fi, cellular and downloads.
  • Original files: FLAC stays FLAC. Or let your server convert to AAC, Opus or MP3.
  • Each level shows how much data an hour of music takes.
  • Formats the iPhone can't play are converted automatically.

Your server, your way

  • Home address: on your Wi-Fi the app talks to the server directly and switches back outside, mid-song and without a gap.
  • Custom HTTP headers per account, for Cloudflare Access, Authelia and similar. Secrets stay in the Keychain.
  • Several accounts, with a “Who's listening?” picker on start.
  • A heart for the lock screen and Control Center (the Control Center button needs iOS 18), and a Siri shortcut to like the current song.

Make it yours

  • Eight accent colors or your own, and nine app icons.
  • Choose the start screen and which rows the home screen shows.
  • Dark gray or plain black, tinted with the cover if you like.
  • Pin playlists to the top of your library with a long press.

Small things

  • The quality badge in the player shows what is playing, like FLAC or AAC 256.
  • Swipe the mini player to skip, swipe up to open the player.
  • Smart playlists from your server are marked and kept read-only.
  • Logging out removes that account's downloads and cache from the phone.

Audiobooks & podcasts

Books and podcasts, in the same app.

Connect your Audiobookshelf server under Settings and your books and episodes sit next to your music. Pick up where you stopped, on any device, because the position goes back to the server.

  • Chapters, speed and a sleep timer. Skip buttons you can set to 10 to 60 seconds, and speeds from 0.75x to 2.5x.
  • Offline. Download books and episodes, listen on a plane, and your position syncs when you're back online.
  • Hearts, authors and series. Favorite books and authors show up in your library, finished books get a check mark.
  • On the watch and in the car. The same controls on your wrist and in CarPlay, and switching between music and a book follows along.

Make it yours

Your colors, your corners.

Tonalo should look like your app. Everything is a tap away under Settings, Appearance, and the app follows at once.

  • Accent color. Eight to pick from, or any color of your own, and nine app icons to match.
  • Background. Dark gray or plain black. Cards, chips and the mini player move along, so they stay readable on both.
  • Corners and covers. Sharp, standard or rounded corners, and small, standard or large covers on the home screen.
  • Your home screen. Choose the start screen and which rows the home page shows.

Radio

Endless radio, and your stations live.

Pick a song and Tonalo keeps playing music like it, straight from your library, for as long as you listen. It gets better the more you skip and like.

The internet radio stations set up in Navidrome play live on both apps. On the iPhone you see the song on air, and if it's in your library, one tap plays it from there.

Between devices

Start on the train, finish on the couch.

Your queue and position are kept on your Navidrome server. Pause on the iPhone, open Tonalo on the TV or PC, and it offers to carry on with the same song at the same second. It works the other way round too. Nothing to set up for this. It uses Navidrome's own play queue.

Pair the iPhone with your TV or PC once under Devices and it does more: while it plays, the phone shows it live, and what you pick on the phone plays there. Nothing goes through the internet.

Jukebox

Friends join with a code, add songs and skip. On the TV or PC at home, or in the car with your iPhone as the host, no Wi-Fi needed. Guests need an account on the same server.

Apple Watch & CarPlay

On your wrist and in the car.

Apple Watch

A remote for the iPhone that comes along with the app. It never talks to your server and stores no login.

  • Player with the cover, play, skip, shuffle and a heart
  • The iPhone's volume on the Digital Crown
  • Start your playlists and favorites from the wrist
  • Audiobooks and podcasts with skip buttons and chapters, and Continue listening first in the library

CarPlay

Your library on the car screen, made to be used at a glance.

  • Home with Continue listening, library and downloads
  • Audiobooks and podcasts, with chapters and speed in the player
  • Shuffle, repeat and a heart in the player
  • No search, because Apple doesn't allow a keyboard in CarPlay music apps

Privacy

Your music stays between you and your server.

Tonalo has no backend of its own. There is no account to create and nothing to opt out of.

No password stored

Only the salted token the Subsonic API uses is kept, never the password itself.

Keychain on the iPhone

The token and any custom headers live in the iOS Keychain, on that one device.

No analytics

No tracking, no ad SDKs, no third-party code at all in the iPhone app.

Headers stay put

Custom headers never follow a redirect to another host.

https by default

Plain http is only used for addresses in your home network.

Open code

Every line is on GitHub, so you can check what the apps do.

The only other connections: a radio station's own server while you listen to it, and your own devices nearby once you pair them. Details in the privacy policy.

  1. Install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store.

LG TV · webOS 5 or newer

LG TV

  1. Rooted TV: in the Homebrew Channel settings, add the repository https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Wzkiie/tonalo/repo/apps.json, then install Tonalo from the list. Updates come the same way.
  2. Developer Mode: download the .ipk from the latest release and install it with webOS Dev Manager.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · Mac with Apple Silicon or Intel

Windows and Mac

  1. Download Tonalo-Setup-….exe (Windows), or the .dmg for your Mac, mac-arm64 for Apple Silicon and mac-x64 for Intel, from the latest release.
  2. Windows: run it. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time: click More info, then Run anyway. Mac: drag Tonalo into Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time, because the app isn't notarized. The Mac app doesn't update itself yet.
  3. Sign in with your server. When the system asks about network access, allow it, so your iPhone can find the computer.

Questions

Good to know

Which Navidrome client works on the iPhone?

Tonalo does. It runs on the iPhone with iOS 17 or newer, with an Apple Watch app and CarPlay. There is no iPad version yet.

Which servers does it work with?

Tonalo is built and tested with Navidrome. Other servers with the Subsonic API, like Gonic or Airsonic-Advanced, should work but haven't been tried yet. Reports are very welcome.

Does it cost anything?

No. All apps are free and MIT licensed, without ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

When will it be in the App Store and the Homebrew Channel?

The iPhone app is in public beta on TestFlight right now; the App Store comes next. The TV app can already be installed from the Homebrew Channel by adding Tonalo's own repository (see above); a listing in the official repository is planned. The .ipk from the GitHub releases also works on any TV in Developer Mode.

Can I try the app without my own server?

Tonalo needs a Navidrome or Subsonic server to play music. To look around first, you can sign in to the public Navidrome demo server at demo.navidrome.org with the user name demo and the password demo. It is run by the Navidrome project, not by Tonalo, and it can change or go offline at any time.

Can I play audiobooks with it?

Yes. Connect your Audiobookshelf server under Settings and your books and podcasts sit next to your music, with chapters, speed and offline downloads.

Can friends add songs to what I play?

Yes, with the Jukebox. Friends join with a code, add songs and skip. They need an account on the same server. It works on the TV or PC at home, and in the car with your iPhone as the host, no Wi-Fi needed.

Why are artist pages empty on my server?

Popular songs, similar artists and biographies come from Navidrome, which gets them from Last.fm. Adding a free Last.fm API key to Navidrome takes two minutes; the server tips show how. Without it, the iPhone app shows your own most played songs instead.

Is there an Android or Samsung TV version?

Not yet. Tonalo is made for LG webOS and the iPhone.

Was AI involved?

A good part of the code was written together with an AI coding assistant. Everything is tested on a real LG OLED and iPhone, and the code is public, so you can see exactly what it does.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Open an issue on GitHub; there are forms for both. On the iPhone you can also send a screenshot as TestFlight feedback. Security problems please report privately.
