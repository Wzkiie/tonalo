Changelog

What's new

Every version of the TV, Windows, Mac and iPhone apps, newest first. The TV, Windows and Mac apps come from the GitHub releases, the iPhone app from the App Store.

  1. TV, Windows & Mac 1.11.4Nov 13, 2026

    DJ on the TV from your iPhone

    • New set. When you steer the DJ from your iPhone while the TV plays, the songs after the current one are replaced by the DJ's new set, as on the phone, instead of being added at the end. Needs the iPhone app 1.5.0 (23).
    Download
  2. TV, Windows & Mac 1.11.3Nov 12, 2026

    Several people on one TV

    • Pairing. If someone else's profile is open on the TV, an iPhone with another account is no longer paired as that profile's own device. It can join the Jukebox instead, or you choose your own profile first. Pair again if a phone ended up on the wrong profile.
    • DJ. The iPhone passes its DJ on only to a TV where its own account is signed in, never into another profile's slot. Needs the iPhone app 1.5.0 (22).
    Download
  3. TV, Windows & Mac 1.11.2Nov 12, 2026

    Connected devices on the TV, sidebar fix

    • TV. A small, permanent note in the sidebar shows which devices are connected right now, for example your iPhone and your MacBook.
    • Mac and Windows. The sidebar was cut off at the bottom while another device was controlled. Fixed, and the library list now scrolls when the window is low.
    Download
  4. TV, Windows & Mac 1.11.1Nov 12, 2026

    The TV in your player, like on the iPhone

    • Mac and Windows: the player follows the other device. While you control a TV or another computer, the player shows what plays there: title, cover, queue and a running progress bar. Previous, play/pause, next, seeking, shuffle and repeat go to that device, and so does everything you start, a song, an album or a playlist, also Play next and Add to queue. Nothing plays on your computer meanwhile.
    • Pairing. The code window is bigger and clearer, and connects as soon as the six digits are in.
    • Other account. If the TV is signed in with a different account, the app says so instead of sending songs it can't play.
    Download
  5. TV, Windows & Mac 1.11.0Nov 12, 2026

    Control the TV from your Mac or PC

    • Mac and Windows: control other devices. Settings, Control other devices: the app finds TVs and other computers with Tonalo on the same network. Pair once with a code, then a bar at the bottom shows what is playing there, with previous, play/pause and next. It is the same pairing the iPhone uses; the keys stay encrypted on your computer.
    • DJ button on the TV. The row of buttons in the player no longer runs into the repeat button when the DJ is set up. The DJ button is set apart in the app's color.
    • Radio. The player hides the heart and lyrics for live radio again, also with the DJ set up.
    Download
  6. iPhone 1.5.0Nov 12, 2026

    The DJ, and many fixes

    • DJ. Optional, off until you set it up: an AI picks sets from your library. Steer it with More like this, Something else, a mood or a wish in your own words. You bring your own key for Anthropic or Google, or use your own Ollama. If you turn on Use on paired devices, your own TV, PC or Mac gets the same setup.
    • CarPlay. A DJ row in Home and Your Library, and a DJ button in Now Playing to steer it.
    • Refreshing. Pull down to refresh in Playlists, Albums, Artists, Search and inside an open playlist. A playlist is up to date right after you add a song, and favorites update after a heart.
    • Network. Switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data no longer repeats a change by mistake, like creating a playlist twice. Saving the library offline tries again after a short network error, and the home address is tried three times before the app gives up on it.
    • Playback. Music continues after a call or Siri. Scrobbles count the time you really listened, so skipping to the end of a song no longer counts.
  7. iPhone 1.4.5Nov 9, 2026

    Jukebox hearts and a simpler queue

    • Jukebox. Songs from a guest show your own hearts as the host, not the guest's. On the host's CarPlay the guest's likes used to show, and the heart switched the wrong way. This applies to an iPhone and to a TV or computer as host.
    • Queue. Hold a song and drag it up or down, no Edit button needed. The song menu stays on the … button.
    • Local network. The permission text now also mentions nearby devices for the Jukebox.
  8. iPhone 1.4.4Nov 6, 2026

    Better on the watch, steadier link to TV and computer

    • Apple Watch. Play and pause on the watch no longer stop the phone by mistake when the watch showed an old state. Covers show up on the watch, and skipping on the phone is shown there too.
    • TV and computer. The phone follows your TV and your computer at the same time and shows the one that is playing. Before, only one of them was followed, so opening the app several times in a row connected only now and then.
  9. TV, Windows & Mac 1.10.0Nov 11, 2026

    Second address and custom headers on the computer, ready for the DJ

    • Mac and Windows: second address. An account can have two addresses, one in your home network and one reachable from outside (Settings, Second address). At home the app uses the home address as soon as the same server answers there, and switches back when you leave, without stopping the song.
    • Mac and Windows: custom headers. Headers for Cloudflare Access and similar proxies can now be changed in the settings, not only when signing in. New values are only saved if the server answers with them.
    • DJ. The TV and computer apps can run the DJ that a coming iPhone version sets up: the iPhone hands its setup to devices paired with the same account, encrypted, if you turn that on. Until then nothing changes.
    Download
  10. TV, Windows & Mac 1.9.1Nov 6, 2026

    Steadier link to the iPhone

    • Mac and Windows. The app starts straight into the account you used last, because the link to the iPhone only exists once an account is open.
    • iPhone link. While no account is open, the app now says so instead of refusing, so the iPhone no longer forgets its pairing.
    Download
  11. iPhone 1.4.3Nov 5, 2026

    Audiobooks on the TV, steadier connection

    • Audiobooks on the TV. A book playing on your TV or computer shows up on the phone, and you control it from there. Play on moves it over from the same spot, in both directions.
    • Steadier connection. The phone finds your TV or computer more reliably after the app opens, also when it was closed while the music kept playing.
  12. TV, Windows & Mac 1.9.0Nov 5, 2026

    Audiobooks, and Tonalo for Mac

    • Audiobooks and podcasts. Connect Audiobookshelf under Settings, or tick the box at sign-in. Chapters, speed, a sleep timer and your progress in sync, and a shortcut on the start page to continue the book.
    • Search finds more. Audiobooks, podcasts, series and authors come up next to your music. Authors have their own page with picture, books and series, and the book page has buttons to the author and the series. Under Audiobooks there is an overview of all series.
    • Together with the iPhone. A book playing on the TV or computer shows up on the phone, with pause, skip, chapters and speed. Play on moves it over from the same spot.
    • Mac. The Windows app now runs on macOS too, as a .dmg for Apple Silicon and one for Intel. It isn't notarized, so the first start needs a right-click and Open, and it doesn't update itself yet.
    • Back button. On the computer, a small arrow at the top left goes back one step from anywhere.
    • The search bar looks calmer. On the TV, audiobooks in the Opus format can't be played by the TV itself; the app says so right away.
    Download

    A song that stays silent without an error goes on as MP3 after a few seconds. Continue from the phone is only offered for a recent queue.

    Download
  15. TV & Windows 1.8.9Nov 5, 2026

    Unplayable formats are converted

    Songs the TV can't play are converted, and the connection to the iPhone is steadier.

    Download
  16. iPhone 1.4.2Nov 5, 2026

    More reliable TV connection

    • TV connection. Songs the TV can't play are converted, a switched-off TV is let go of sooner, and it's found again by itself.
    • Starting over. A song you stopped long ago starts from the beginning. Audiobooks still continue where you were.
  17. iPhone 1.4.1Nov 4, 2026

    Faster downloads, audiobooks on the watch and in the car

    • Apple Watch and CarPlay. Audiobooks and podcasts show up on the watch and in the car, and switching between music and a book follows along.
    • Faster downloads. Big playlists download much faster, and the app stays smooth while they do.
    • Look. Dark gray instead of pure black. Under Settings, Appearance you can go back to black and change the corner shape and cover size.
    • Recently played. The library can be sorted by what you listened to last.
    • Continue listening now follows the book that is playing, tapping the open tab again scrolls to the top, and seeking in music works again after an audiobook.
  18. iPhone 1.4.0Nov 3, 2026

    Audiobooks and podcasts from Audiobookshelf

    • Audiobooks and podcasts. Connect your Audiobookshelf server under Settings and listen in the same app, with chapters, speed, a sleep timer and your progress in sync.
    • Listen offline. Download books and episodes just like music. Your position goes to the server as soon as there's a connection again.
    • Your favorites in the library. Give books, podcasts and authors a heart and they show up in Your Library. Finished books have a check mark and a paler cover.
    • Authors and series. Open an author to see their books and series, sort them, and switch between tiles and a compact list.
    • At home and on the go. Audiobookshelf can have an address for outside and one for home, plus custom headers for Cloudflare Access and similar.
    • Tapping the open tab again clears its filter, and the player and tabs no longer sit on top of the keyboard.
  19. iPhone 1.3.0Nov 1, 2026

    Play on your computer, and Devices and Jukebox apart

    • Play on your computer. Pair Tonalo for Windows under Devices and it works like the TV: the iPhone shows what's playing there and sends music to it. The PC shows up with a computer icon.
    • Devices and Jukebox. Your own TV or computer is under Devices; hosting a Jukebox and joining one have their own page.
    • Favorites offline. Your favorite songs open at once without a connection, from the saved copy or your downloads.
    • What's New now shows one thing per page to tap through, and the version history opens version by version.
  20. TV & Windows 1.8.8Nov 3, 2026

    Windows: servers behind Cloudflare Access

    Sign-in now has custom headers, with a one-click preset for a Cloudflare Access service token. They go with every request to your server, including music and cover art, and are stored encrypted on the PC. The TV app is unchanged.

    Windows: a new switch, Visible on the network, hides the PC from iPhones, for example on hotel or café Wi-Fi. The app also blocks scripts that aren't its own.

  25. TV & Windows 1.8.3Nov 1, 2026

    The iPhone finds the PC

    • Fixed: the iPhone couldn't connect to Tonalo on a Windows PC that also has virtual networks (WSL, Hyper-V, VPN). The PC now always tells it the address in your home network.
    • The PC shows up as PC (or the name you gave it) instead of DESKTOP-…, and music it left on the server is offered as coming from the PC, not the TV.

  26. TV & Windows 1.8.2Nov 1, 2026

    Tonalo for Windows

    The TV app now also runs on Windows 10 and 11, with the same screens and features, made for mouse and keyboard. Download Tonalo-Setup-1.8.2.exe from the release; after that, updates install from inside the app.

    • Volume slider in the player and in full screen, also for radio stations. Scroll on it to change the volume.
    • Click in the progress bar to jump, right-click for options, Space to play and pause. Media keys and the Windows media overlay work.
    • Your iPhone controls it and can host a Jukebox on it, just like on the TV.
    Download
  27. TV 1.8.1Oct 31, 2026

    Join the Jukebox when someone else is signed in

    If another person has chosen their account on the TV, your iPhone shows Join next to the TV. Tap it and you're a guest of their Jukebox, without a new code. When your account is back on the TV, your iPhone controls it as usual again.

    • Fixed: an iPhone lost its pairing when someone else signed in on the TV.
    • Fixed: the link to the iPhones could stop when the account changed while someone was connected.
    Download
  28. iPhone 1.2.0Oct 31, 2026

    Play on your TV, Jukebox and a better CarPlay

    • Play on your TV. While your TV plays Tonalo, the iPhone shows what's playing there, and music you pick plays on the TV. Pair once under Settings → TV & Jukebox.
    • Choose where it plays. The TV button in the full-screen player moves the music to the TV or back to the iPhone, from the same spot in the song.
    • Jukebox. Friends join with a code, add songs and skip – on the TV at home, or in the car with your iPhone as the host, no Wi-Fi needed.
    • Better CarPlay. A Radio tab, colorful mixes, and shuffle, repeat and the heart show when they're on. A song that drops out goes on where it stopped.
    • Pairing never sends the code over the network, and every request is signed so it can't be replayed.
  29. TV 1.8.0Oct 31, 2026

    Play together with your iPhone

    • See what's playing on the iPhone – song, queue and controls – and pick music there that plays on the TV.
    • Move the music from the iPhone to the TV and back, from the same spot.
    • Jukebox. Friends nearby join with a code shown on the TV, add songs and skip. They don't see whose account is playing, and the TV briefly shows who added what.
    • The TV and the iPhone talk to each other directly in your home network. Nothing goes through the internet.
